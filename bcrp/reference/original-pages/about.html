About

Dr. B. C. Roy Polytechnic, Durgapur,opened its doors in 2014 under the umbrella of Dr. B. C. Roy Engineering College Society.  This college is the brainchild of a group of Durgapur based philanthropic entrepreneurs. Dr. B. C. Roy Polytechnic was created in the memory of Dr. Bidhan Chandra Roy, the architect of modern West Bengal. The management is  highly committed to transform the institute into one of the leading  educational departments in Eastern India. Dr. B. C. Roy Polytechnic engineering institute keeps in mind the global as well as domestic corporate requirements for quality personnel. 

Being affiliated to the West Bengal State Council of Technical and Vocational Education and skill development it caters to the student as well as industry requirements. The institute is approved by the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE). The courses we offer at Dr. B. C. Roy Polytechnic, are in tandem with the latest syllabus as structured by the West Bengal State Council of Technical and Vocational Education and skill development. Our job includes grooming and training the students to sculpt out future leaders of industry. We are dedicated to providing quality education. We are ready to face all the possible challenges and overcome them with confidence to let BCRP stand tall along with the premier institutions offering Diploma Engineering Program. 

Affiliations and Approvals 

BCRP offers Diploma courses in Civil Engineering (CE), Computer Science and Technology (CST), Mechanical Engineering (ME), Electrical Engineering (EE) and Electronics and Telecommunication Engineering (ETCE) that are in coherence to the latest syllabus structured by WBSCTE along with affiliations and approvals as required.

  • Approved by AICTE, New Delhi
  • Affiliated to the West Bengal Council of Technical Education
  • Approved by the Department of Higher Education, Government of West Bengal
